Halo Pulse

Personal wellness prototype. Not a medical device, and not an emotion reading — see PLAN.md.

Before you start

  • Your camera is processed only in memory, in this browser tab. Video is never uploaded, recorded, or sent anywhere.
  • Only derived numbers (heart rate, tension estimates, a stress index) are ever saved — never a frame, image, or face template.
  • This is a personal self-tracking tool: not a diagnosis, not an emotion reading, not for use on anyone but yourself.
  • You can revoke consent, export, or delete everything at any time.

Calibration

Sit still, relax, and look at the camera for about a minute. This builds your personal baseline — everything else is measured as a change from this, not an absolute number.
